What Bible Teaching Looks Like Here

At The Gathering, every sermon is rooted in the Scripture text. We preach expositionally -- we open the Bible, work through it passage by passage, and let it set the agenda. We believe the Bible is the inspired, inerrant Word of God and the authority for everything we do.

We have preached through Ephesians and are building a congregation that knows, loves, and lives the Word. If you have been looking for a church in Johnson County that takes the Bible seriously and trusts the Spirit to make it alive, you belong here.
