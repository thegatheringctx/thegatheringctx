Who we are
in Cleburne, TX.

The Gathering CTX is a new church in Cleburne, Texas, planted in March 2026. We started in a barn in Blum with a handful of families and one conviction: the church exists to make disciples, not fill buildings.

We are not the biggest church in Cleburne. We are not trying to be. We are a family on mission. A community where you do not just attend, you belong. Where you do not just show up, you become.

Every Sunday at 5 PM we gather at the Cleburne Conference Center for worship, the Word, and community. Doors open at 4:30 PM. Come as you are. There is a seat at the table for you.

If you have been searching for a church in Cleburne, TX that takes the Bible seriously, welcomes real people, and believes discipleship actually changes lives, come check us out this Sunday.

What to expect at
The Gathering CTX.

Service Times
Every Sunday at 5:00 PM. Doors open at 4:30 PM. We start on time and end on time. About 75 minutes total.
Location
Cleburne Conference Center, Cleburne, TX 76033. Large parking lot. Easy to find. Arrive by 4:45 PM for a good spot.
What We Teach
Scripture-driven, Spirit-led preaching. We teach directly from the Bible. You will open your Bible every Sunday. No fluff.
Atmosphere
Casual dress. Real people. No performance culture. We value the Word more than the show. Come as you are.
Families & Kids
Babies through Pre-K have their own room. Ages 5 and up stay in the main service and get their Kids Zone packet after worship.
First Visit
You will not be called out, asked to stand, or pressured to give. Come in. Take a seat. Just experience it.
