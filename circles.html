Circles at The Gathering

Open toeveryone.

A women's group. A men's group. A season of life shared around the Word and around each other.

Circles are open groups built around a shared interest or a season of life, led by someone with a heart to gather people. Whether you are a partner, a regular, or brand new, there is a Circle with your name on it.

I  ·  What Circles Are

Community, open to all.

Circles are one of the ways The Gathering lives out a simple conviction: people grow in the context of real relationship. Being for one another, with one another, and present to one another is not a ministry strategy. It is obedience.

A Circle is open to anyone and built around a shared interest or a season of life, led by someone with a heart to gather people around the Word and around each other. You do not have to be a partner. You do not have to have it all together. You just have to come.

III  ·  The Table and Circles

Two expressions of the same heart.

At The Gathering, community takes more than one shape. The Table is the covenant anchored expression: every partner belongs to one, with a shepherd, a rhythm, and a pastoral care layer. It is the promise made to every partner.

Circles are another expression of the same heart, open to everyone and built around a shared interest or season of life. A person may be part of both, and many are. One does not replace the other. The Table keeps the promise to partners. Circles open the door to everyone.

Community is not optional here. It is who we are.
