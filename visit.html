First Time Here?

Let's plan it,
one step at a time.

Visiting a new church can feel like a lot. Take it a tap at a time. Here are the only things you actually need to know before Sunday.

What to Expect

No dress code.
No performance.
Just show up.

We are a new church and proud of it. There is no established culture you have to decode. No insider language to learn first. Just people who are hungry for God and serious about following Jesus. You will fit right in.

Atmosphere
Warm. Real. Grounded.

Casual dress, genuine people, no production fog. We value the Word more than the show.

Music
Worship that means something.

Live worship that points to Jesus. Not a performance. An invitation.

The Message
Scripture-driven. Spirit-led.

You will open your Bible. You will hear the Word taught with conviction and clarity. No fluff.

Duration
About 90 minutes.

We start on time and finish on time. We value your Sunday evening.

Kids
Families are welcome.

Bring your family as you are. We are a young church still building our kids ministry. Reach out and we will make sure your family is taken care of.

Questions
people
actually ask.

We know first visits can feel like a lot. Here are the most common things people want to know before you walk through the door.

What do I wear?

Whatever you put on that day. We mean it. Jeans, work clothes, Sunday best. None of it matters. You matter.

Do I have to give money?

No. We do not pass a plate or make a moment out of giving. Generosity here is voluntary and joyful. New here? Keep your wallet closed.

Will I be put on the spot?

No. We will not ask you to stand up, introduce yourself, or raise your hand. Come in, take a seat, and just experience it.

I have not been to church in a long time.

Good. Then you are exactly who this place was built for. Come. Sit down. See what God does.

Where do I park?

The Cleburne Conference Center has a large parking lot. Arrive by 4:45 and you will have no trouble finding a spot.

Can I bring a friend?

Please do. That is exactly what we are here for.
