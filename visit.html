Questions
people
actually ask.

We know first visits can feel like a lot. Here are the most common things people want to know before you walk through the door.

What do I wear?

Whatever you put on that day. We mean it. Jeans, work clothes, Sunday best. None of it matters. You matter.

Do I have to give money?

No. We do not pass a plate or make a moment out of giving. Generosity here is voluntary and joyful. New here? Keep your wallet closed.

Will I be put on the spot?

No. We will not ask you to stand up, introduce yourself, or raise your hand. Come in, take a seat, and just experience it.

I have not been to church in a long time.

Good. Then you are exactly who this place was built for. Come. Sit down. See what God does.

Where do I park?

The Cleburne Conference Center has a large parking lot. Arrive by 4:45 and you will have no trouble finding a spot.

Can I bring a friend?

Please do. That is exactly what we are here for.

WHAT TO EXPECT

No surprises.
Just welcome.

We know visiting a new church can feel uncertain. Here is exactly what you will walk into when you show up.

How long is the service?

About 75 to 90 minutes. We start at 5:00 PM and doors open at 4:30 PM. Come early and meet people.

What should I wear?

Whatever you want. Jeans, boots, a dress, a hoodie. There is no dress code here. Come as you are. That is not a slogan, it is our actual expectation.

What is the worship like?

Contemporary worship music, led live. It is participatory, not performative. We sing to God, not for an audience. You are free to engage however feels natural.

What about my kids?

Kids are welcome in the service with you. We also have the Kids Gathering Zone, a dedicated experience for children. Ask at the door and we will get them connected.

Do I have to give?

Absolutely not. Giving is for those who call The Gathering home. If you are visiting, just show up. You are our guest.

Where do I park?

The Cleburne Conference Center has ample free parking. Pull into any open spot. You will see the signs directing you inside.
