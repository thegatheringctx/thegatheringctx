🏪 Ages 4–7 · Week 6

God’s Superhero Armor! ⚔️

5 days with your family · Ephesians 6

“Finally, be strong in the Lord and in the strength of his might.”

Ephesians 6:10, ESV

⭐ Read together with your grown-up and earn +10 points each day! ⭐
1

Day 1

Listen and Love Your Mom and Dad

❤️

📖 The Bible Says

“Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right. ‘Honor your father and mother.’”

Ephesians 6:1–2, ESV

Did you know that God has helpers? God made moms and dads to help take care of you. When you listen to your mom or dad, you are showing love to GOD too. That is a really big deal!

Honoring your mom and dad means more than just doing what they say. It means treating them like they matter. Because they do! It means being kind even when you do not feel like it.

Here is something cool: God made a special promise that goes with this. When we honor the people God gave us, things go well. That is a promise straight from God himself!

💬 Let’s Talk About It

  • What is one way you can show love to your mom or dad today?
  • Can you think of a time when listening helped things go better?

⚡ Say It Out Loud!

I honor my mom and dad because I love God! I listen and I am kind! I walk with Jesus, and THAT means I win!

Amen! 🎉

✍️ Family Activity

Draw a picture of you and your mom or dad doing something fun together. Write “I honor you!” at the top.

👁 Parent Note

After the declaration, give your child a big hug and say: “God is so proud of you.” This reinforces that honor is relational, not just behavioral.

2

Day 2

Do Your Best. Even When Nobody Is Watching.

⭐

📖 The Bible Says

“...doing the will of God from the heart, rendering service with a good will as to the Lord and not to man.”

Ephesians 6:6–7, ESV

Have you ever done a really good job at something and nobody noticed? Maybe you cleaned up your toys but nobody said “great job.” Maybe you were kind to someone and nobody saw.

Here is the secret: GOD always sees. God sees every good thing you do, even when nobody else does. He is keeping track. And He loves when you do your best. Not because someone is watching. Because you love Him!

Paul wrote something funny in this part of the Bible. He called it “eye-service.” That is a made-up word that means: only doing a good job when someone is watching. Paul said: do not do that! Do your very best ALL the time. Because you are really doing it for God.

💬 Let’s Talk About It

  • Can you think of a job at home you can do with your whole heart this week?
  • How does it feel to know God always sees when you do good things?

⚡ Say It Out Loud!

I do my best all the time because God always sees me! I work for God, not just for people! I walk with Jesus, and THAT means I win!

Amen! 🎉

✍️ Family Activity

Draw yourself doing one job at home really well. Write “For God!” at the top. Put it on the fridge so you can see it all week!

👁 Parent Note

This week, catch your child doing something good when they think no one is watching. Remind them: “I saw that. And God saw that too.”

3

Day 3

God Is Bigger Than Everything Scary

🛡️

📖 The Bible Says

“Finally, be strong in the Lord and in the strength of his might.”

Ephesians 6:10, ESV

Did you know there are things we cannot see that sometimes try to make us afraid or sad or angry? The Bible calls these the enemy. And the enemy is real. But here is the most important thing: GOD IS BIGGER. Way, way, way bigger.

Paul says to be strong. But not in your own strength. In GOD’S strength. That means when you are scared or worried or sad, you do not have to handle it alone. You can ask God for His strength. And God’s strength NEVER runs out.

Think of it like this. If a really really big dog was coming toward you and you were scared, and then your dad stepped in front of you and said “I’ve got you.” You would not be scared anymore! That is what God does. He steps in front of every scary thing and says: “I’ve got you.”

💬 Let’s Talk About It

  • Is there something that makes you feel scared or worried?
  • What can you say to God when you feel afraid?

⚡ Say It Out Loud!

God is bigger than anything scary! I am strong because God makes me strong! I walk with Jesus, and THAT means I win!

Amen! 🎉

✍️ Family Activity

Take a piece of paper and write something scary on it. Then draw a big cross over it. Say together: “God is bigger than this!”

👁 Parent Note

Pray with your child right now about whatever they mentioned. Let them hear you pray specifically for their worry by name. It shows them prayer is real and personal.

4

Day 4

God Made You Special Armor!

⚔️

📖 The Bible Says

“Take up the whole armor of God, that you may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand firm.”

Ephesians 6:13, ESV

Superheroes have special gear. Capes, shields, helmets. Did you know that God made special armor just for YOU? It is not armor you can touch. But it is real. And it protects you!

🟢 The Belt of Truth — knowing what is true about God and about you. You are loved. You belong to God!

⚫ The Breastplate of Righteousness — Jesus made you right with God. You are clean because of Jesus!

👣 The Shoes of Peace — God’s peace goes with you everywhere you walk!

🛡️ The Shield of Faith — believing what God says even when things feel hard!

👑 The Helmet of Salvation — knowing you belong to God now and forever!

📑 The Sword of the Spirit — the Bible! When you say what the Bible says, it is like swinging a sword against lies!

💬 Let’s Talk About It

  • Which piece of armor is your favorite? Why?
  • Can you name all six from memory?

⚡ Say It Out Loud!

I wear God’s armor every day! I have truth and peace and faith and salvation! The Bible is my sword and I will use it! I walk with Jesus, and THAT means I win!

Amen! 🎉

✍️ Family Activity

Draw a picture of yourself wearing God’s armor. Try to label as many pieces as you can! Belt, breastplate, shoes, shield, helmet, sword.

👁 Parent Note

Each morning this week, do a quick “armor check” with your child. “Belt of truth on? Shield of faith raised?” Make it fun and quick.

5

Day 5

Talk to God Every Day. Prayer Is Your Radio.

🙏

📖 The Bible Says

“...praying at all times in the Spirit, with all prayer and supplication.”

Ephesians 6:18, ESV

Have you ever seen a walkie-talkie? To talk to someone on a walkie-talkie, both radios have to be on. If you turn yours off, you cannot hear the other person. Prayer is like keeping your radio on with God.

Paul says pray at ALL times. That means morning when you wake up. Before meals. When you are scared. When you are happy. At night before bed. When something good happens. When something hard happens. All the time. Always.

God loves to hear your voice. He is never too busy. He never has bad signal. He never puts you on hold. When you talk to God, He listens every single time.

And here is something cool. Paul, the man who wrote this whole book of the Bible, asked people to pray for HIM too! Even the biggest Christians need other people to pray for them. So pray for your family. Pray for your friends. Pray for your church!

💬 Let’s Talk About It

  • What is one thing you want to tell God right now?
  • Who is someone you can pray for today?

⚡ Say It Out Loud!

I talk to God every day! He always hears me! I pray for my family and my friends! I walk with Jesus, and THAT means I win!

Amen! 🎉

✍️ Family Prayer

Pray this together right now: “God, thank You for loving me. Thank You for giving me armor. Thank You for always listening. Help me be strong in You every single day. I love You. In Jesus’ name, Amen!”

👁 Parent Note

You made it through all five days! This week let your child hear you pray out loud. Let them see that you talk to God too. That models faith more than anything.
