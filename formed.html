The Course

The goal is not that
you finish a course.

The goal is that by the time you are done, you are standing on something that does not move.

Formed and Sent takes the six first principles of Hebrews 6:1 to 2 off the page and into the bones of your life. The writer of Hebrews calls these elementary doctrine. That does not mean beginner and it does not mean optional. It means foundational. The stones everything else gets built on.

Hebrews 6:1 to 2, ESV
"Therefore let us leave the elementary doctrine of Christ and go on to maturity, not laying again a foundation of repentance from dead works and of faith toward God, and of instruction about washings, the laying on of hands, the resurrection of the dead, and eternal judgment."

"The person who never had these properly laid is always one storm away from a collapse they cannot explain."

Formed & Sent · Hebrews 6:1 to 2
Foundations for Discipleship

The five weeks.
Stone by stone.

Six first principles from Hebrews 6:1 to 2, one foundation at a time. By the last week you are not just informed. You are sent.
Week 01
The Ground Beneath You
Repentance from dead works and faith toward God

Repentance is not condemnation. It is realignment. We look at what repentance actually is, what it is not, and why grace is the only thing that ever closes the gap.

Matthew 7:24 to 27 · Psalm 51 · Hebrews 11:1
Week 02
Buried and Raised
Instruction about washings (baptisms)

The plural in Hebrews 6:2 is doing real work. Baptism into the body of Christ, baptism in water, and baptism in the Holy Spirit. This is also where we say plainly where we stand on the Word and the Spirit. We are unapologetically both.

Romans 6:3 to 4 · Acts 2:38 · Acts 1:8
Week 03
Hands That Carry Heaven
The laying on of hands

Ministry is not what professionals do on a stage while everyone else sits in rows. You are a carrier. Before this session ends, somebody will lay a hand on your shoulder and name what they see in you.

Genesis 2:7 · 1 Timothy 4:14 · James 5:14 to 15
Week 04
The Day Everything Is Settled
The resurrection of the dead and eternal judgment

People brace for this week and then find it is the most hope filled one in the course. You are already raised. You are already seated. There is no condemnation. And nothing you do for Him is ever wasted.

1 Corinthians 15 · Ephesians 2:6 · Romans 8:1 · 2 Corinthians 5:10
Week 05
Formed. Now Sent.
Discipleship and multiplication

You were not saved to sit. You leave this week knowing how to actually disciple somebody, with the words to say and the questions to ask, and you leave commissioned. Cleburne is the mission field and you are already in it.

Matthew 28:18 to 20 · 2 Timothy 2:2 · Luke 9:23
