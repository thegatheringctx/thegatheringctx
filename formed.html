The Course

Not a class.
A foundation.

Formed & Sent is a five-week discipleship course built on the six First Principles of Hebrews 6:1–2. Every week, one stone. By the time you finish, you will have a rock-solid, personal answer to the question every storm eventually asks.

What is actually underneath your life?

Not a textbook answer. Your own. Built from the Word, tested in prayer, and ready to be passed on to the next person who needs it.

Five Weeks at a Glance

Stone by stone.
Week by week.

Each week builds on the last. Each stone is irreplaceable. By Week 5 you will not just know what The Gathering believes you will be standing on it.
Week 01
The Sure Foundation
Repentance from Dead Works · Faith Toward God

What you are building on determines everything. Two builders. Same storm. One standing, one in ruins. The difference was never strength it was always foundation. This week you lay the first two stones.

Matthew 7:24–27 · Psalm 51 · Hebrews 11:1
Week 02
Baptized into New Life
The Doctrine of Baptisms

The Christian life is not an improved version of your old life. It is an entirely different one. This week unpacks three baptisms into the Body of Christ, into water, and into the power of the Holy Spirit and why every believer needs all three.

Romans 6:4 · Acts 1:8 · Acts 2:38
Week 03
Hands That Carry Heaven
Laying on of Hands

What God placed in you is meant to flow through you into the lives of people around you. This week uncovers the five functions of laying on of hands and why your hands are one of the primary mechanisms the Kingdom operates through.

Genesis 2:7 · 1 Timothy 4:14 · James 5:14
Week 04
Resurrection Life
Resurrection of the Dead · Eternal Judgment

You do not strive toward victory. You live from it. This week confronts the two foundation stones that create the most urgency in a disciple's life and the most hope. Everything rises or falls at the empty tomb.

Romans 8:11 · Ephesians 2:6 · 2 Corinthians 5:10
Week 05
Become and Multiply
Discipleship: Go, Baptize, Teach

You were not saved to sit. You were saved to be sent. The final week puts a commission on everything you have built. Foundation leads to formation. Formation leads to multiplication. This is where it all goes.

Matthew 28:18–20 · 2 Timothy 2:2
