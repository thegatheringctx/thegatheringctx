What you will
experience Sunday.

When & Where
Every Sunday at 5:00 PM
Cleburne Conference Center, Cleburne TX
Doors open 4:30 PM
Atmosphere
Casual dress. Real people. No performance. We value the Word more than the show.
The Message
Scripture-driven. Spirit-led. Practical. Bring your Bible or pull one up on your phone. You will use it.
Duration
About 75 minutes. We start on time and end on time. We value your Sunday evening.
Kids
Babies through Pre-K have their own room at drop-off. Ages 5 and up stay in the main service, then get their Kids Zone packet after worship.
First Visit
You will not be called out, asked to stand, or pressured to give. Come in. Take a seat. See what God does.
