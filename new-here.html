What to Expect

A Sunday at
The Gathering.

Here’s what a typical Sunday looks like — not to prepare a performance, but so you can walk in and simply be present.

4:30
Doors Open
Come early. Meet people. There’s no rush. The half hour before is one of the best parts — real conversation, no agenda.
5:00
We Gather
We open in worship. Songs rooted in Scripture, sung together. Not a performance — a declaration. Everyone in the room participates.
Word
The Message
We preach the full counsel of Scripture. Practical, grounded, and unapologetic. Every week anchored in a text and applied to real life.
Table
Communion
We take communion together every week. It is open to all who are in Christ. Not a ritual — a declaration of what He finished.
Prayer
We Pray
Prayer is not a formality here. We believe it changes things. We pray for each other, for Cleburne, and for what God is building.
Stay
After Service
Nobody rushes out. We linger, we talk, we connect. Some of the best moments happen in the 20 minutes after the service ends.
The Setup

No stage.
No platform.

Chairs are set up in the round — the worship team and the pastor are in the middle, and the community gathers around them. Not an audience watching a stage. A family around a table.

There is no separation between the people leading and the people gathered. We are all in the room together. That is not an accident. That is the conviction.

What you walk into on Sunday is less like a concert and more like a living room. Warm, real, and fully present.

They devoted themselves to the apostles’ teaching, to fellowship, to the breaking of bread, and to prayer.
Acts 2:42
Children

Kids belong
here too.

Faith is caught as much as it is taught. We believe children grow when they are in the room with the community — not separated from it.

Ages 4 & Under
Dedicated Care Space
We offer a dedicated space for children four and under so parents can engage fully in worship. Our team is ready to love on your little ones while you are in the room.
Ages 5 & Up
In the Room With Us
Children five and older join us in the gathering. They are welcome, expected, and loved. If they make noise, that is okay — we are a family, not a performance. Parents are the primary disciple-makers for their children. We come alongside you.
“For as often as you eat this bread and drink the cup, you proclaim the Lord’s death until he comes.”
1 Corinthians 11:26
Every Week

We take
communion together.

Every Sunday, we observe the Lord’s Supper together. It is not a ritual or a formality. It is a declaration — a proclamation of what Christ finished on the cross, made together as a body.

Communion is open to all who are in Christ. If you are His, there is a place at this table. If you are still figuring that out, you are welcome to watch and reflect. No pressure.

Ready to
come see for yourself?

You do not need to have it together. You do not need to know anyone. You just need to show up. The rest takes care of itself.
