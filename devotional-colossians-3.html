Colossians: Rooted / All of Him · Week 3 Devotional

Christ In You, The Hope of Glory

The treasure is a Person, and He lives in you.

Colossians 1:24 to 2:5

The false teachers in Colossae traded in one thing. Secret knowledge. A hidden wisdom for the inner circle. A mystery the ordinary believer had not reached. So watch what Paul does with their favorite word. He takes mystery and he detonates it.

Yes, he says, there is a mystery, hidden for ages. But it is not hidden anymore, and it is not a formula or a password. It is a Person. Christ in you, the hope of glory. Five days this week we stop hunting for something higher and learn to draw on the Christ we already carry.

01

The Mystery Is a Person

the mystery hidden for ages and generations but now revealed to his saints... which is Christ in you, the hope of glory.

Colossians 1:26 to 27 · ESV

The false teachers traded in secrets, a hidden wisdom for the inner circle. Paul answers with one sentence that ends the whole hunt. Yes, there is a mystery, hidden for ages. But it is not hidden anymore, and it is not for sale, and it is not for the few. It has been made known to His saints. All of them.

And what is behind the curtain? Not a diagram. Not a discipline. Christ in you, the hope of glory. The living God does not dwell only in a temple far off or a heaven above. By His Spirit He has taken up residence in ordinary people who believed. Read the last three words slowly. The hope of glory. Because Christ lives in you now, glory is coming for you later.

Greek Word: mystērion (μυστήριον), a mystery, not a riddle you solve but a truth God kept concealed and has now openly unveiled. The very word the false teachers used to gatekeep, Paul throws open to everyone. Made known to His saints, all of them. So stop hunting for a hidden higher life. The secret is out, and the secret has a name.

Reflect:
1. Do you live like Christ actually dwells inside you, or like He is far off?
2. What next thing have you been chasing to feel close to God?
3. If the treasure already lives in you, what changes about today?

Reflect

Have you been hunting for something God has already announced out loud?

Say it with your name in it. Christ is in me, and He is my hope of glory. What does that settle today?

02

There Is No Room Down the Hall

Him we proclaim, warning everyone and teaching everyone with all wisdom.

Colossians 1:28 · ESV

We still line up for the secret. The next conference that will finally unlock it. The teacher with the deeper revelation. The podcast, the download, the anointing you have not caught yet. We keep treating Jesus like the lobby and the real thing like a room down the hall we have not been let into.

Paul says there is no room down the hall. The fullness moved into you the day you believed. Notice how he preaches. Warning everyone, teaching everyone. Not a private class for the advanced. The whole message, for the whole church, in the open. There is no inner ring. There is only Christ, and Christ is fully yours. You are not missing a piece. You are learning to live from the One you already have.

Reflect:
1. Where have you believed you were spiritually behind everyone else?
2. What would it look like to stop chasing and start resting this week?
3. Who around you feels like they are on the outside? How could you tell them the secret is out?

Reflect

What have you treated as the lobby to something better than Jesus? The next conference, the next teacher, the next anointing you have not caught yet.

What changes if you stop looking past Him?

03

Maturity, Not Membership

...that we may present everyone mature in Christ.

Colossians 1:28 · ESV

Count the word everyone in this passage. Three times. Warning everyone. Teaching everyone. That we may present everyone mature. There is no elite tier in Paul's ministry. And notice the goal. Not to make people impressive. To make them mature. Whole. Grown up in Jesus.

So stop measuring yourself against some secret spiritual elite. God is not making you impressive. He is making you whole. And here is the mercy in it. He will finish what He started. On the days you feel least mature, He is still the One at work, and He does not abandon a project halfway.

Greek Word: teleios (τέλειος), mature, complete, whole, brought to the intended goal. The aim of the gospel is not a higher class of Christian. It is a finished one. Membership gets you in the room. Maturity is Christ formed in you, day by ordinary day, until you are whole. And He will complete it.

Reflect:
1. Are you trying to be impressive, or letting God make you whole? What is the difference in your life?
2. Where have you been discouraged because growth felt slow?
3. What does it change to know He will finish what He started?

Reflect

Are you aiming at impressive or at mature? They are not the same thing.

Where are you still a beginner with God, and will you let Him grow you there instead of hiding it?

04

God's Energy in a Spent Body

For this I toil, struggling with all his energy that he powerfully works within me.

Colossians 1:29 · ESV

Paul works hard. He toils. He struggles. This is not an easy, effortless faith. And yet the power under his effort is not his own. That is the secret of every tired servant of God. You pour out. You labor. You spend yourself. And underneath your effort runs a power that is not yours.

So if you are running on empty today, worn down from serving, wondering how long you can keep giving out, hear this. Do not quit. The One who calls you to the labor supplies the energy for it. You are not asked to manufacture strength you do not have. You are invited to work from a power that never runs dry.

Greek Word: energeia (ἐνέργεια), working, energy, effective power in operation. Paul toils by the energeia that powerfully works within him. It is God's own energy at work in the worker. When you are spent in serving, remember whose power is carrying it. The God who assigns the work supplies the fuel.

Reflect:
1. Where are you running on your own strength and need to be refilled?
2. What have you been trying to sustain that only God can sustain?
3. How would today feel different if you worked from His energy instead of yours?

Reflect

Where are you spending yourself on your own fuel and calling it faithfulness?

Before your hardest work this week, say out loud whose energy you are working in.

05

Love That Labors in Secret

For I want you to know how great a struggle I have for you... in whom are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge.

Colossians 2:1 to 5 · ESV

Paul is writing from a Roman prison. In chains. And he says he has a great struggle for people he has never even met, faces he had never seen. From a cell where no one could watch, he agonized in prayer for strangers. That is what real love does. It labors in secret for people who will never know.

And what does he want for them? Hearts encouraged, knit together in love, settled into the full knowledge of Christ, in whom are hidden all the treasures. Which is why he writes so that no one may delude you with plausible arguments. The lie does not arrive looking like a lie. It arrives sounding reasonable. Smooth. So the strong church is not the one with the most secrets. It is the one with the firmest faith, so rooted in Christ that a smooth argument cannot move it.

Greek Word: agōn (ἀγών), a struggle, a contest, an agony; the word for an athletic or military fight. It gives us the word agony. Paul's prayer for believers he had never seen is a wrestling match, not a formality. Measure your love by what it will labor for in secret. Who are you wrestling for on your knees?

Reflect:
1. Who is God asking you to labor for in secret this week, with no credit?
2. What plausible argument has been quietly pulling you off Christ?
3. Where do you need your faith to be more firm and settled this week?

Reflect

Who has labored for you in prayer in a room you never saw?

Pick one person and fight for them this week in a way that will never get credited to you.

Reflect on the Week

Where have you believed you were spiritually behind everyone else?

Are you trying to be impressive, or letting God make you whole?

Who is God asking you to labor for in secret this week, with no credit?

A Prayer of Identity

Father, thank You that the mystery of the ages is not a secret I have to earn. It is Your Son, and He lives in me. I stop hunting for a higher room. I stop treating Jesus like a lobby. You are not building a spiritual elite. You are making ordinary people whole, and I am one of them, and You will finish what You started. When I labor, work Your energy in me and not my own. Root me so deep in Christ that no smooth argument can move me. In Him are hidden all the treasures, and He is mine. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Declaration

Christ is in me, and He is my hope of glory.

The mystery has been revealed, and the treasure lives inside me.

I am not chasing a secret. In Christ are hidden all the treasures, and Christ is mine.

When I labor, I labor in His energy, not my own.

I do not add to Jesus. I draw all of life from Jesus.

I walk with victory, not to it, in Jesus' name. Amen.

Now go, and carry the treasure. May you stop hunting for a secret and rest in the Christ who lives in you.

Now to him who is able to strengthen you according to my gospel and the preaching of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the mystery that was kept secret for long ages but has now been disclosed and made known to all nations, to bring about the obedience of faith, to the only wise God be glory forevermore through Jesus Christ! (Romans 16:25 to 27)

In the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. Amen.
