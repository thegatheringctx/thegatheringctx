Decrees.Say what is true.

A decree is not a wish. It is not a bargain with God. It is you agreeing out loud with what He has already said about you. We do not declare in order to become. We declare because we already are. Identity first. Behavior follows.

1 and 2 Peter · Living Hope · Aug 30 to Oct 18 2026

I have been born again to a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ.

My inheritance is imperishable, undefiled, and unfading, kept in heaven for me.

By God's power I am guarded through faith, so I rejoice even in the fire.

I was ransomed, not with silver or gold, but with the precious blood of Christ.

I am a living stone, a chosen race, a royal priesthood, His own.

I cast all my anxiety on Him, and the God of all grace will strengthen and establish me.

I walk with victory, not to it, in Jesus' name. Amen!

Line by line, this is the letter of Peter: born again to a living hope through the resurrection (1:3), the inheritance imperishable, undefiled, and unfading, kept in heaven (1:4), guarded by God's power and rejoicing in the fire (1:5 to 7), ransomed not with silver or gold but with the precious blood of Christ (1:18 to 19), a living stone and a royal priesthood who are His own possession (2:5, 2:9), casting all anxiety on Him because the God of all grace restores and establishes (5:7, 5:10), and victory already won in His name.

How to use it

Read it out loud. That is the whole instruction. Speak it in the car, over your kids, in the room where the fear usually wins. We read this together every week of the series, right after the Scripture reading, until the room knows it by heart.

If you are new to this, start with the first line and stay there a week. Born again. A living hope. Your hope is not tied to your circumstances improving. It is tied to a Christ who is alive. Everything else in this decree grows out of that ground.
