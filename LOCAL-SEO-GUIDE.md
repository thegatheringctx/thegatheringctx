# Local SEO and Google Business Profile

This is the practical playbook for showing up when someone in Cleburne or
Johnson County searches "church near me," "churches in Cleburne TX," or
"non denominational church." The website already carries the technical
signals Google reads (see the bottom section). The biggest remaining lever
is your **Google Business Profile**, which is the free listing that appears
in Google Maps and in the box on the right of search results. That part
lives in your Google account, not in this repo, so it is the one piece I
cannot edit for you. This guide walks you through it.

Everything below uses the same name, address, and phone the website uses.
Keeping these identical everywhere is the single most important local SEO
rule, so copy and paste rather than retyping.

## 1. Claim and verify the profile

1. Go to `https://business.google.com` and sign in with the church Google
   account (the same one that owns the domain and email if possible).
2. Search for "The Gathering CTX." If a listing already exists, click
   **Claim this business**. If nothing shows, choose **Add your business**.
3. Category matters a lot. Set the **primary category** to `Church`. Add
   these as **additional categories**: `Non-Denominational Church`,
   `Christian Church`. Categories are the strongest ranking signal in the
   whole profile, so get these right.
4. Verify. Google will offer phone, email, or a mailed postcard. Verify as
   soon as the option appears. Nothing you edit goes live until you do.

A note on the address: because you meet at the Cleburne Conference Center
rather than a building the church owns full time, list the address and, if
Google asks, mark that you serve customers at your business address. If a
staff member or reviewer questions the shared venue, that is normal for
churches that rent space. Keep the address consistent with the website.

## 2. Fill out every field

Google rewards completeness. Do all of these:

- **Description** (750 characters). Suggested text:
  > The Gathering CTX is a Spirit led, non denominational church in
  > Cleburne, Texas. We gather Sundays at 5:00 PM at the Cleburne
  > Conference Center for worship, Bible teaching, and real community.
  > Kids ministry is provided for babies through 5th grade. Come as you
  > are. New here? Everything you need to know is at gatheringctx.org.
- **Hours**. Add Sunday `4:30 PM to 6:30 PM`. Leave the other days closed
  unless you add a weekday event. This matches the website and the schema.
- **Opening date**. Set the month and year the church launched. This helps.
- **Phone**. `(682) 286-5640`. Use the same number as the website.
- **Website**. `https://gatheringctx.org`.
- **Appointment / menu links**. Point the primary action link to
  `https://gatheringctx.org/visit`. You can add more links: giving to
  `https://gatheringctx.org/give`, and new here to
  `https://gatheringctx.org/new-here`.
- **Services / attributes**. Turn on the ones that apply: kids ministry,
  wheelchair accessible entrance and parking, restroom, free parking,
  identifies as, and welcoming attributes. These show as badges and match
  real searches.
- **Social links**. Add Facebook and Instagram (same handles as the site).

## 3. Photos (this moves the needle more than people expect)

Profiles with photos get meaningfully more clicks and direction requests.
Upload real photos, not stock:

- A clear **logo** and a **cover photo** (a strong worship or room shot).
- 8 to 15 photos of the gathering, worship, the kids area, the welcome
  table, the building exterior so people recognize it when they arrive,
  and a few smiling faces (with permission).
- Add a few new photos each month. Freshness is a ranking signal, and the
  gallery on the website (`/gallery`) is a good source.

## 4. Get reviews the honest way (and never fake them)

Reviews are the second biggest ranking factor after categories, and they
are what a first time visitor actually reads. Two rules: only ask real
people who have really been, and never buy, trade, or write fake reviews.
Beyond being against Google's policy, it is not who the church is.

A simple, repeatable ask:

1. Create a short review link. In the profile dashboard choose **Ask for
   reviews** and copy the short link Google gives you (it looks like
   `https://g.page/r/...`). 
2. Shorten it to something memorable, or make a QR code, and put it on a
   small card at the welcome table.
3. Two or three times a year, from the platform, send a warm note: "If The
   Gathering has been a home to you, a short Google review helps another
   family in Cleburne find us." Include the link.
4. **Reply to every review**, good or hard. Thank people by name. For a
   critical one, respond with grace and an invitation to talk. Google
   watches owner responses, and visitors watch how you handle criticism.

Once you have a handful of genuine Google reviews, tell me and I will add
verified review and rating structured data to the website so the stars can
appear in search results. I am not adding any rating markup until the
reviews are real, because fabricated ratings violate Google's guidelines
and can get the whole site penalized.

## 5. Keep it alive

- **Posts**. Once a week or so, publish a Google post (they behave like
  short social posts on your listing): this Sunday's message, an event, a
  new devotional. It signals an active, real church.
- **Q&A**. Seed the Questions section yourself with the real questions from
  the website FAQ (denomination, service length, kids, what to wear,
  where). You can ask and answer from the owner account.
- **Events**. Add special services (Easter, Christmas, baptisms, a sermon
  series kickoff) as events on the profile.

A profile that is claimed, complete, photographed, reviewed, and posted to
will out rank a bare listing almost every time, even against larger
churches.
