Every Question Answered

What do I wear? Whatever you are comfortable in. Jeans, boots, dresses -- come as you are.

How long is service? About 90 minutes. We start at 5:00 PM and finish around 6:30. We start and end on time.

What about my kids? Babies through Pre-K have a dedicated room. Kids 5 and up stay with you through worship, then head to Kids Zone during the message.

Where do I park? Free parking at the Cleburne Conference Center. Our team members will help you find a spot.

Do I have to give? Not at all. The offering is for our church family. Guests never feel pressure.

What denomination are you? Independent, non-denominational. Evangelical and continuationist -- we believe the gifts of the Spirit are active today.

What is worship like? Live music, genuine, and participatory. Scripture-rich songs. Come ready to encounter God.

Will anyone pressure me? No. You can observe and leave without anyone chasing you down. We respect where you are.

Come as you are.
Leave formed.
