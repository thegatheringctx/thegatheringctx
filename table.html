The Table Ministry

TheTable.

You were not meant to do this alone. And now you do not have to.

The Table is the pastoral care and discipleship rhythm of The Gathering. Every covenant partner belongs to one, so that no partner is unknown, unprayed for, or unaccounted for. It is not a program. It is a promise kept.

What It Is

A small group where you are known.

A Table is a small group of partners who gather around an open Bible, prayer, and life together, often over a meal. Every partner belongs to one, so no one falls through the cracks of a growing church.

Each Table is led by a host who knows you by name, prays for you, and shows up before you have to ask.

A Gathering

What a night looks like.

  1. Arrive and catch up, often over a meal.
  2. Worship. A few songs, a moment to turn toward God.
  3. Open the Word, often building on Sunday's message.
  4. Talk it through. Questions, honesty, real life.
  5. Pray for one another's needs.
  6. Leave more known than when you arrived.
Rhythm
At least twice a month.
Size
A small group with one host.

Kids are part of the point, not a problem to solve. The door stays open to anyone.

Your Seat

Every partner has a Table.

Sign up for the Table that fits your life, your side of town, and the night you can actually make. If you do not choose one, we will place you in one, thoughtfully. Nobody in this family goes without a Table.

Looking for something built around a shared interest or a season of life instead? That is a Circle, and it is open to everyone.
