Blessed Before
You Breathed

The Staggering Grace of Election and Spiritual Blessing

Ephesians 2:13-22

You were far off. You were a stranger. You had no covenant, no hope, no access to God. And then everything changed.

"But now in Christ Jesus you who once were far off have been brought near by the blood of Christ." Ephesians 2:13

The Distance Was Real

Paul does not soften the reality of what you were before Christ. You were dead in your trespasses. You walked according to the prince of the power of the air. You were by nature children of wrath. The distance between you and God was not a misunderstanding — it was a chasm.

But God did not meet you halfway. He crossed the entire distance. In the flesh and blood of Jesus Christ, the far-off were brought near. The wall of hostility was torn down. The dividing line between Jew and Gentile, between holy and unholy, was abolished in His body on the cross.

Peace Is a Person

"For he himself is our peace." Ephesians 2:14

Peace is not a feeling Paul is promising. It is not a circumstance. Peace is a person — Jesus Christ — who in His own flesh made both groups one. This is not the peace of tolerance or coexistence. This is the peace of reconciliation, purchased at the price of His own blood.

You are not at peace with God because you got better. You are at peace with God because Jesus stood in your place, absorbed the wrath you were owed, and gave you access to the Father that you could never earn.

Access to the Father

"For through him we both have access in one Spirit to the Father." Ephesians 2:18

The word "access" here is the same word used for an audience with a king. You have been ushered into the presence of God — not as a stranger, not as an outsider, but as a member of His household. You are no longer a foreigner. You are a fellow citizen with the saints, built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, with Christ Jesus himself as the cornerstone.

What This Means for You Today

You were far. You are near. Not because of anything you did — but because of what He did. Let that sink in today. The blood of Christ is not just your entry ticket into Christianity. It is the ongoing basis of your nearness to God every single moment.

When you pray today, you are not talking to a distant God. You are speaking to a Father who brought you near at infinite cost to Himself. Come boldly. Come near. The distance has been abolished.

"So then you are no longer strangers and aliens, but you are fellow citizens with the saints and members of the household of God." Ephesians 2:19

A Prayer for Today

Father, thank You for not leaving me at a distance. Thank You that the blood of Your Son was sufficient to bring me all the way near — to make me a member of Your family, not a stranger at the door. Help me to live today from the nearness You have given me, not the distance I used to know. In Jesus' name, Amen.
