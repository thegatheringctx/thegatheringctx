    How It Works
    Everything you need to know about the Gathering Kids Zone
    For Kids
    ⚡
    Sign Up
    Tap I'm a Kid on the home screen. Enter your first name, pick a 4-digit PIN with your parent, and choose your age group. Done!
    🎯
    Earn Points
    Check in daily, read devotionals, take Bible quizzes, and play games. Every action earns points toward real prizes.
    🎉
    Earn Badges
    Badges unlock automatically as you grow - streaks, points, quizzes, devotionals, and summer challenge milestones all count.
    🎁
    Redeem Prizes
    Use your points in the Prize Store - stickers, books, t-shirts, camp scholarships, and even lunch with Pastor Billy.
    For Parents
    👤
    Sign Your Child Up First
    Have your child tap I'm a Kid and register with a first name plus a 4-digit PIN you both choose. Write the PIN down - you will need it.
    🔒
    Log In to the Parent Portal
    Tap Parent on the home screen. Enter your child's PIN to see their points, streaks, badges, and devotional progress.
    👪
    Multiple Kids?
    Once inside the portal, tap Find Another Child by PIN. Enter each child's name and PIN to link them all to your account.
    📖
    Family Devotionals
    Every week's kids devo is tied to Sunday's sermon. Find them under Family Devos. A printable 6-week Ephesians booklet is also available.
