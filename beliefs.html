Statement of Faith

What We
Believe.

The convictions that anchor everything at The Gathering CTX. Not a checklist -- a foundation. We hold these truths from Scripture with joy, humility, and conviction.

Adopted by the Board of Directors of Gathering in Victory Inc.  ·  March 9, 2026

I  ·  The Holy Scriptures

The Holy Scriptures

The Holy Bible, sixty-six books of the Old and New Testaments, is the inspired, inerrant, and infallible Word of God. Scripture alone is the supreme and final authority in all matters of faith and practice.

We preach through books of the Bible verse by verse because we believe the text has the agenda. Our job is not to use Scripture to say what we want -- it is to let Scripture say what God wants. It is alive, active, and more than enough.

Key Scripture

"All Scripture is breathed out by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness."

2 Timothy 3:16–17

"Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path."

Psalm 119:105

II  ·  The Nature of God

The Nature of God

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, co-equal, co-eternal, co-substantial. Creator and Sustainer of all things, worthy of all worship.

God the Father is sovereign, holy, loving, and just. He is not distant -- He is near. Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man, born of a virgin, sinless in life, crucified for our sin, bodily risen, ascended, and returning in glory. The Holy Spirit is fully God, active and present today.

Key Scripture

"Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit."

Matthew 28:19

"For in him the whole fullness of deity dwells bodily."

Colossians 2:9

III  ·  Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ

The eternal Son of God, born of a virgin, lived sinlessly, died as a substitutionary atonement for sin, rose bodily from the dead, ascended to the right hand of the Father, and will return personally and visibly in power and glory.

There is salvation in no one else. Jesus is not one way among many -- He is the way, the truth, and the life. His resurrection is the foundation of our faith and the guarantee of our victory.

Key Scripture

"And there is salvation in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given among men by which we must be saved."

Acts 4:12

"But thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ."

1 Corinthians 15:57

IV  ·  The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit

We are continuationist. We believe all the gifts of the Holy Spirit described in Scripture are active and available to the Church today. The same Spirit who moved in Acts is moving now.

The Holy Spirit convicts of sin, regenerates the lost, indwells every believer, and empowers the Church for mission. We make room for prophecy, healing, tongues, and every gift described in 1 Corinthians 12–14. The Spirit and the Word always work together. We test everything by Scripture -- but we will not tell the Spirit He is done doing what He said He would do.

Key Scripture

"The Helper, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, he will teach you all things and bring to your remembrance all that I have said to you."

John 14:26

"Earnestly desire the spiritual gifts."

1 Corinthians 14:1

V  ·  Humanity & Sin

Humanity & Sin

Humanity was created in the image of God -- with dignity, worth, and purpose. Through Adam's disobedience, all people are born with a sinful nature, in need of redemption only possible through faith in Jesus Christ.

Sin is not just bad behavior. It is a condition of the heart that separates us from God and leads to death. Every person is a sinner in need of grace. No one is beyond the reach of that grace.

Key Scripture

"For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God, and are justified by his grace as a gift, through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus."

Romans 3:23–24

VI  ·  Salvation

Salvation

Salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone. It is the gift of God, received through genuine repentance and faith in Jesus Christ. It cannot be earned, inherited, or achieved.

All who believe are justified, born again, and granted eternal life. Salvation is not just a transaction -- it is a transformation. The one who is saved is being made new and walking in the victory Christ has already won.

Key Scripture

"For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God, not a result of works, so that no one may boast."

Ephesians 2:8–9

VII  ·  Baptism

Baptism

We practice baptism by immersion as a public declaration of faith -- an outward sign of an inward transformation. Baptism does not save, but it is an act of obedience and a bold public statement that you belong to Jesus.

We baptize believers, not infants. If you have trusted Jesus and have never been baptized, we would love to talk with you about it. It is one of the most powerful moments in the life of a follower of Christ.

Key Scripture

"We were buried therefore with him by baptism into death, in order that, just as Christ was raised from the dead by the glory of the Father, we too might walk in newness of life."

Romans 6:4

VIII  ·  The Lord's Supper

The Lord's Supper

We observe the Lord's Supper regularly as a remembrance of Christ's body broken and blood shed for us. It is a proclamation of His death until He comes again -- a moment to re-center, re-orient, and re-commit to following Him.

It is open to all who have trusted in Jesus Christ as Savior and Lord. We do not take it lightly. We take it with gratitude.

Key Scripture

"For as often as you eat this bread and drink the cup, you proclaim the Lord's death until he comes."

1 Corinthians 11:26

IX  ·  The Church

The Church

The universal Church is the body of Christ, made up of all who have been redeemed by Him. The local church is God's primary vehicle for making disciples, proclaiming the gospel, and advancing His kingdom.

The Gathering CTX is an elder-led, independent, non-denominational church. We hold a high view of local church membership, community, accountability, and mission. We love the whole Church and partner broadly with the Body of Christ.

Key Scripture

"And they devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and the fellowship, to the breaking of bread and the prayers."

Acts 2:42

X  ·  Marriage

Marriage

We believe marriage is a covenant between one man and one woman, as established by God in creation and affirmed by Jesus in the Gospels. It is not a social contract or cultural arrangement -- it is a sacred covenant designed to reflect the relationship between Christ and His Church.

We hold this conviction with love, not condemnation. Every person, regardless of background or struggle, is made in the image of God, loved by God, and welcome at The Gathering. Grace is not the absence of truth. Truth is not the absence of grace.

Key Scripture

"Therefore a man shall leave his father and his mother and hold fast to his wife, and they shall become one flesh."

Genesis 2:24

"Have you not read that he who created them from the beginning made them male and female?"

Matthew 19:4

XI  ·  Life

Life

We believe human life is sacred from conception to natural death. Every person, at every stage and in every condition, bears the image of God and is therefore worthy of dignity, protection, and love.

This conviction shapes how we think about the unborn, the elderly, the vulnerable, and every person on the margins. A church that walks in victory walks in the defense of life -- because the Author of life calls us to.

Key Scripture

"For you formed my inward parts; you knitted me together in my mother's womb. I praise you, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made."

Psalm 139:13-14

"So God created man in his own image, in the image of God he created him; male and female he created them."

Genesis 1:27
