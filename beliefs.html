Statement of Faith

What We
Believe.

These are the convictions that anchor everything at The Gathering CTX. Not a checklist -- a foundation. Adopted March 9, 2026.

01

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, inerrant, and infallible Word of God. Every word of Scripture -- both Old and New Testaments -- is breathed out by God and is therefore fully trustworthy and authoritative in all that it affirms.

Scripture is the final authority for what we believe and how we live. It is not just historically reliable or spiritually helpful -- it is the living Word of the living God, and it has the power to transform every person who receives it with faith.

We preach through books of the Bible verse by verse because we believe the text itself has the agenda. Our job is not to use Scripture to say what we want to say -- it is to let Scripture say what God wants to say.

Key Scriptures

2 Timothy 3:16–17 2 Peter 1:20–21 Psalm 119:105 Hebrews 4:12 John 17:17

02

God -- Father, Son, and Holy Spirit

We believe in one God who exists eternally in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Each Person is fully God, distinct in role, united in essence. The Trinity is not a theological puzzle -- it is the nature of the God who made us, saved us, and dwells in us.

God the Father is sovereign over all creation. He is holy, loving, and just. He is not distant -- He is near, and He is good.

Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man. He was born of a virgin, lived a sinless life, died on the cross as the atoning sacrifice for sin, rose bodily from the dead on the third day, ascended to the right hand of the Father, and will return in glory. There is no other name by which we must be saved.

The Holy Spirit is fully God. He convicts of sin, regenerates the lost, indwells every believer, and empowers the Church for mission. He is not merely a force -- He is a Person, and He is present and active today.

Key Scriptures

Matthew 28:19 John 1:1–14 John 14:16–17 2 Corinthians 13:14 Colossians 2:9 Acts 4:12 1 Corinthians 15:3–4

03

Salvation

We believe that every human being is made in the image of God, but that all have sinned and fallen short of His glory. Sin is not just bad behavior -- it is a condition of the heart that separates us from God and leads to death.

Salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone. It cannot be earned, inherited, or achieved. God, in His mercy, sent His Son to bear our sin on the cross. Those who repent and trust in Jesus are forgiven, justified, and adopted into God's family.

Salvation is the beginning of a new life -- not just a transaction but a transformation. The one who is saved is being made new, conformed to the image of Christ, and walking in the victory He has already won.

Key Scriptures

Romans 3:23 Romans 6:23 Ephesians 2:8–9 John 3:16–17 Romans 10:9–10 2 Corinthians 5:17 1 Corinthians 15:57

04

The Holy Spirit and Spiritual Gifts

We are continuationist. We believe all the gifts of the Holy Spirit described in 1 Corinthians 12–14 are active and available to the Church today. The same Spirit who moved in Acts is moving now.

We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit as an empowering work of the Spirit in and for the life of the believer. We make room for prophecy, healing, tongues, and all the gifts described in Scripture -- not as spectacle, but as Spirit-empowered means of building up the body and advancing the kingdom.

We hold these convictions with humility. The Spirit and the Word always work together. We test everything by Scripture. But we refuse to be cessationist -- to tell the Spirit He is done doing what He said He would do.

Key Scriptures

1 Corinthians 12:4–11 Acts 1:8 Acts 2:1–4 1 Corinthians 14:1 Romans 8:26–27 Ephesians 4:11–13 1 Thessalonians 5:19–21

05

The Church

We believe the universal Church is the body of Christ, made up of all who have been redeemed by Him. The local church is God's primary vehicle for making disciples, proclaiming the gospel, and advancing His kingdom on earth.

The Gathering CTX is an elder-led, independent, non-denominational church. We love the whole Church and partner broadly with the Body of Christ. We hold a high view of local church membership, community, accountability, and mission.

We practice two ordinances: water baptism (by immersion, as a public declaration of faith) and the Lord's Supper (regularly observed as a remembrance of Christ's body and blood until He returns).

Key Scriptures

Matthew 16:18 Ephesians 4:11–16 Acts 2:42–47 1 Timothy 3:1–13 Matthew 28:18–20 Romans 6:3–4 1 Corinthians 11:23–26

06

Marriage and Family

We believe marriage is a covenant between one man and one woman, as established by God in creation and affirmed by Jesus in the Gospels. Marriage is designed to reflect the covenant relationship between Christ and His Church.

We believe human beings are created male and female, in the image of God. Biological sex is a gift and a design, not an identity to be redefined or rejected.

We hold these convictions with love, not condemnation. Every person -- regardless of background, history, or struggle -- is made in the image of God, loved by God, and welcome at The Gathering.

Key Scriptures

Genesis 1:27–28 Genesis 2:24 Matthew 19:4–6 Ephesians 5:25–33 1 Corinthians 7:2

07

The Return of Christ and Eternity

We believe in the personal, bodily return of Jesus Christ. He will return in glory, as He promised, to judge the living and the dead and to make all things new.

Those who have trusted in Christ will spend eternity with Him in the new creation -- a place of perfect joy, wholeness, and the unhindered presence of God. Those who have rejected Christ will face eternal separation from God.

This is not a minor doctrine. It shapes how we live, how we love, and how we preach. Every sermon we preach has eternity in the room. We are not trying to make people comfortable -- we are calling them to a decision that matters forever.

Key Scriptures

John 14:3 Acts 1:11 1 Thessalonians 4:16–17 Revelation 21:1–5 Matthew 25:31–46 Hebrews 9:27–28
