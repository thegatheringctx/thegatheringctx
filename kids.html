Safety & Security

Your child's safety
is not an afterthought.

We take child protection seriously. Every policy exists because your children matter to God and they matter to us.

✅
Background Checked Volunteers

Every Kids Zone volunteer undergoes a background check before working with children.

🔒
Secure Check-In & Pickup

Matching number tags for drop-off and pickup. Your child is only released to the person with the correct tag.

👥
Two-Adult Rule

No child is ever alone with a single adult. At least two adults are present at all times.

📱
Parent Contact

If your child needs you during service, we reach you immediately. We have your number and we will use it.

Questions
parents
always ask.

We want you to walk in with confidence. Here are the most common questions from first-time families.

Do kids go to a separate room?

No. Your whole family stays together in the main service. After worship, kids pick up their Kids Zone packet at the table and engage with age-appropriate content during the message.

What is in the Kids Zone packet?

Each bag or packet has age-appropriate activities, Scripture, and content tied to what is being preached. Kids stay engaged while you stay present.

What curriculum do you use?

We teach directly from Scripture, tied to the Sunday message when possible so families can continue the conversation at home.

My child has special needs. Can they participate?

Yes. Email us before Sunday at info@gatheringctx.org so we can make sure your child is supported well.

Are snacks provided?

Please let us know about any allergies at check-in. We want every child safe and comfortable.

The Kids Gathering Zone App

Discipleship does not
stop on Sunday.

The Kids Gathering Zone is not a kids app. It is a discipleship engine built for the next generation. Here is what your child steps into every time they open it.

⭐
Points Portal

Earn 5 to 50 points per day for Scripture reading, devotionals, and Bible challenges. Points are real and redeemable.

📖
Bible Quiz

Daily Bible quiz. 10 points for every correct answer. Kids show up knowing the Word because they have been in it all week.

🔥
Daily Streaks

Check in every day and build your streak. Consistency is a discipline. We build it early and make it rewarding.

💕
Family Devotionals

Weekly devotionals tied directly to Sunday's sermon. Read together as a family. Earn bonus points for “We did this together.”

🆕
Prize Store

Points turn into real prizes. Including camp money. Kids have a reason to stay engaged every single day of the week.

📚
Big Questions

Real answers to real questions from Scripture and history. No sign-in needed. Open to every kid who has ever wondered “is this real?”

🔐 Parent Portal

You stay in the loop. The Parent Portal lets you see what your child is earning, reading, and learning. PIN-protected. Your kids' progress is visible to you always.

⚔️ Warrior Avatars

Kids build their warrior identity. Avatars reflect their Gathering ID and grow as they grow. Identity rooted in who God says they are, not the world.

⚡
Scripture Challenge: Free to Play

No sign-in needed. Any kid can jump in and test their Bible knowledge right now. No barriers. Just the Word.
