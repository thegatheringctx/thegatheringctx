What to Expect

Here is what happens
when your family walks in.

01
Arrive Together

Your whole family walks in together. Doors open at 4:30 PM. Everyone worships in the same room. No separation at the door.

02
Worship as a Family

Kids stay with you through worship. They experience the presence of God alongside their family. That is intentional.

03
Kids Zone Table

After worship, kids head to the Kids Zone table to pick up their bag or packet for the day. Age-appropriate content tied to the Sunday message.

04
Message Time

Kids engage with their packet during the message. Same Word, different delivery. Families leave having encountered the same truth.

Safety & Security

Your child's safety
is not an afterthought.

We take child protection seriously. Every policy exists because your children matter to God and they matter to us.

✅
Background Checked Volunteers

Every Kids Zone volunteer undergoes a background check before working with children.

🔒
Secure Check-In & Pickup

Matching number tags for drop-off and pickup. Your child is only released to the person with the correct tag.

👥
Two-Adult Rule

No child is ever alone with a single adult. At least two adults are present at all times.

📱
Parent Contact

If your child needs you during service, we reach you immediately. We have your number and we will use it.

Questions
parents
always ask.

We want you to walk in with confidence. Here are the most common questions from first-time families.

Do kids go to a separate room?

No. Your whole family stays together in the main service. After worship, kids pick up their Kids Zone packet at the table and engage with age-appropriate content during the message.

What is in the Kids Zone packet?

Each bag or packet has age-appropriate activities, Scripture, and content tied to what is being preached. Kids stay engaged while you stay present.

What curriculum do you use?

We teach directly from Scripture, tied to the Sunday message when possible so families can continue the conversation at home.

My child has special needs. Can they participate?

Yes. Email us before Sunday at info@gatheringctx.org so we can make sure your child is supported well.

Are snacks provided?

Please let us know about any allergies at check-in. We want every child safe and comfortable.

Ready to bring
your family?

We would love to meet your family this Sunday. Doors open at 4:30 PM. Everyone worships together.
