What to expect

Not an orientation.
An invitation.

Belong & Become is not a membership class. It is not a list of rules or a tour of the building. It is a vision class. A chance to sit in the room, hear the heart behind everything we are building, and decide if this is where you belong.

By the time you leave, you will know who we are, what drives us, how we are structured, and exactly what your next step looks like. No pressure. No obligation. Just clarity.

Some people walk out ready to call this home. Others need more time. Both are okay. The table stays open.

01
The Vision
Why The Gathering exists, what we are building, and the convictions we will not negotiate.
02
The Structure
How we are built, what we stand on, and the rhythm of life together: large gatherings and small.
03
Your Next Step
What partnership at The Gathering actually looks like, and what we are committing to you in return.
04
Q&A + Prayer
The floor is yours. Ask anything. We close together in prayer.
