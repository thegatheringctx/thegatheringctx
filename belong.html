The Table and Circles

Two names for
two different things.

The Table is care. Every covenant partner has one. Sign up for the one that fits your life, your side of town, and the night you can actually make. If you never get around to picking, we place you, on purpose and with prayer. Nobody in this family goes without a Table.

Your Table Leader is a shepherd, not a coordinator. Somebody who has committed to know you, pray for you, notice when you go quiet, and show up before you have to ask. It gathers at least twice a month around a meal, worship, the Word, and prayer. Kids are part of the point, not a problem to solve. The Table does not end.

A Circle is interest. You choose it. Men, women, recovery, young families, a book, a study, a season of life. It runs for a season, and it is allowed to finish.

If it can end when the study ends, it is a Circle. If it should still be standing there when your life falls apart on a Tuesday, it is a Table.

Guests are invited to the table. Partners are given one.
A note on the change
What you have known as Small Gatherings is now a Circle. Same room, same people, clearer name. We stopped using one word for two different things.
