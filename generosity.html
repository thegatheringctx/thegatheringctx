“Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.”

2 Corinthians 9:7
What We Believe

Not a dead work.
A joyful response.

Generosity is not a dead work driven by guilt, performance, or the need to earn God’s approval. It is a joyful response flowing from hearts transformed by repentance and faith the foundation of a Word-driven life.

Everything we do is made possible through generosity. Every life reached, every disciple formed, every need met in Cleburne and beyond is a result of people who give freely and joyfully.

We don’t give because we have to. We give because God has already given.

Who We Are
Formed by the Word. Led by the Spirit. Walking in the victory Christ has already won.
We are a family on mission disciples who multiply the Kingdom through cheerful, faith-filled generosity.
This is who we are: A people whose giving is worship. A people whose generosity reflects Jesus.
And with every act of generosity, we declare: “I walk with victory, not to it, in Jesus’ name.”
