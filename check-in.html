Kids Check-In Now Fully Digital

Check in from your phone.Skip the paper.

Starting this Sunday, you check your kids in right from your phone before service, and pickup is a quick, secure scan. Here is how to get set up in about two minutes.

Sundays 5:00 PM · Cleburne Conference Center
Before Sunday

Get set up in two minutes

Three quick things at home, then you are ready. You only do this once.

1

Download the Church Center app

It is free, and it is the same trusted system The Gathering already uses for everything. Made by Planning Center.

iPhoneApp Store, search "Church Center"
AndroidGoogle Play, search "Church Center"
2

Sign in and connect to The Gathering

Open the app and follow along. Takes less than a minute.

aFind your church. Search The Gathering or gatheringctx.org, then tap us when we appear.
bSign in with your email. Use the same email you gave us when you first visited. Not sure which one? Ask us and we will look it up in about thirty seconds.
cCheck that your kids are listed. Tap the menu, then Profile, then Household. Your children should appear under your name. If anyone is missing, just let a team member know.
3

Check your kids in on Sunday

Do this on the way to church or right when you walk in.

aOpen Church Center and tap Check-In. Your kids show up under your household.
bTap each child to check them in, choose Kids Zone as the location, and tap Save.
cA security code appears on your screen automatically. That is your pickup pass, so keep it handy.
After Service

Pickup is fast and secure

No paper tag to dig for. Your phone is the pass.

1

Walk to the Kids Zone

Our volunteer will be at the door to greet you.

2

Open Church Center and tap Check-In

Your security barcode appears. The app switches to check-out mode automatically once your kids are checked in.

3

Hold up your phone for the scan

The volunteer scans your barcode on the iPad, it matches you to your kids, and you are checked out. Done.

Good to know

If someone other than you is picking up, let our volunteer know ahead of time and they can verify by name.

No problem. Our volunteer can look up your child by name on the iPad.
