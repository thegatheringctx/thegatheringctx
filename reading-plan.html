Reading Plan.Before Sunday.

Five days of reading that walk you into the passage before it is preached, and one day to sit in it again after. You will arrive on Sunday already familiar with the text. That changes what the message can do in you.

Six short readings a week, Monday through Saturday. Nothing longer than a chapter. The Saturday reading is always the Sunday passage, read straight through one more time.

Then on Monday, the devotional picks up where the message left off. Read the plan, hear the Word, walk it out.
