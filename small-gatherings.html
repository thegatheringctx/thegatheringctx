Small Gatherings

Faith grows best
in close quarters.

Sunday gathers the community. Small gatherings are where the community actually happens. Real people, real life, real faith.

Why Groups

You were not meant to
do this alone.

The early church did not just gather once a week and scatter. They ate together, prayed together, carried each other's weight, and walked each other through real life. That is the model we are building from.

Small gatherings at The Gathering are not programs. They are relationships. People who know your name, your kids, your struggles, and your story. People who will still be there when Sunday is a long way off.

Groups are forming now. If you are ready to go deeper, reach out and we will connect you.

“And they devoted themselves to the apostles’ teaching and the fellowship, to the breaking of bread and the prayers.”
Acts 2:42
What to expect

What small gatherings
actually look like.

Rooted in the Word
Every group studies Scripture together. Not a lecture. A conversation around what God is actually saying and how it applies to your real life this week.
Honest Prayer
We pray for each other. Not performance prayer. The kind where you actually tell people what is going on and trust them with it.
In Homes
Groups meet in people’s homes around Cleburne. Smaller rooms, longer tables, and the kind of conversation you can not have in a crowd.
Real Relationships
You will know people’s names. You will know their kids. You will celebrate with them and carry weight with them. That is the point.
Regular Rhythm
Groups meet consistently, typically every other week. Enough to build something real without it feeling like another obligation on the calendar.
Open to All
You do not need to have it together to join a group. You need to want community. That is the only requirement. The table is open.
