What We Believe

Four Convictions.

01

We Are Formed by the Word

Scripture is not just a guide — it is the living, active Word of God that shapes who we are. We preach through books of the Bible, verse by verse, because we believe God's Word is sufficient to do what only God's Word can do.

02

We Are Led by the Spirit

The Holy Spirit is not a historical figure. He is present, active, and moving today. We make room for the Spirit to lead in worship, in prayer, and in the life of our community. Word and Spirit work together.

03

We Walk in Victory

Victory is not something you earn — it is something Christ has already won. We live from that reality, not toward it. "But thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ." 1 Corinthians 15:57

04

We Make Disciples

We exist to make disciples who are confident and empowered to make disciples. Every person who walks through our doors is a potential world-changer. Our job is to form them, equip them, and send them.
