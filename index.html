What We Believe

Four Convictions that shape everything we do.

01

The Bible is the living Word of God

We preach through books of the Bible verse by verse because we believe Scripture is alive, authoritative, and more than enough. It sets the agenda for everything.

2 Timothy 3:16–17

02

The Holy Spirit is active today

We are continuationist. The gifts of the Spirit are not historical relics. The same Spirit who moved in Acts moves now. We make room for Him in worship, prayer, and life.

1 Corinthians 12:4–11

03

Every believer is a disciple-maker

The Great Commission is not for professionals. Every follower of Jesus is called, equipped, and sent to make disciples. We exist to raise up people who raise up people.

Matthew 28:19–20

04

You walk with victory, not toward it

Christ has already won. The Christian life is not striving toward victory -- it is learning to walk in the victory that already belongs to you in Him.

1 Corinthians 15:57

How We Live

Four Foundations that shape our life together.

01

Word-Driven

The Word defines truth and anchors the Church against drift. Everything we do flows from what Scripture actually says -- not what we wish it said, not what culture demands. The Word has the final word.

02

Spirit-Led

All gifts of the Holy Spirit are active and available today. The Spirit empowers obedience and alignment. We do not operate in our own strength -- we move when He moves and trust what He does.

03

Prayer-Fueled

Prayer is relational dependence that sustains everything we do. We pray first, not last. Not as a formality -- as a lifeline. A church that stops praying stops moving with God.

04

Fully Activated

A victorious Church is built on activated sons and daughters. Every believer carries presence and proclamation. Ministry is not reserved for the platform -- it belongs to every person in the room.
