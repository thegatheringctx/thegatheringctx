Philippians

Joy, contentment, and the mind of Christ  ·  Coming Summer 2026

Philippians is a letter written from prison by a man who had lost everything, and was full of joy. Paul did not write about the possibility of joy. He wrote from the middle of it. That is what makes this book unlike anything else in the New Testament.

For people who have learned to walk with victory rather than toward it, Philippians is the handbook. It is not theory. It is testimony. It is a man who had found the secret of contentment in Christ telling you exactly what it looks like.

We are preaching through it verse by verse. Come ready to walk away with something that changes Monday morning.
