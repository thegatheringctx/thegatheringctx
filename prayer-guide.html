Prayer Guide.Pray the letter.

This is not a prayer list that never changes. Each series gives us a direction to pray in. We pray the letter we are preaching. When Peter tells a suffering church what is already true of them and how to stand, we pray it back, for ourselves, for our families, and for Cleburne.

Three movements. Take one a day and cycle through the week, or pray all three in one sitting. There is no wrong way. The point is that your praying and your hearing are pointed at the same thing.

1 and 2 Peter · Living Hope · Aug 30 to Oct 18 2026

For yourself

1 Peter 1:3 to 9 · 1 Peter 5:6 to 7

Peter does not open by telling a suffering church to try harder. He opens with what God has already done. He caused you to be born again into a living hope, He is keeping an inheritance for you, and He is guarding you by His own power. The praying starts there, in what is already true, not in what you are afraid of.

So do not begin with the fire. Begin with the hope. Set your hope fully on the grace that is coming, name the trial for what it is, proving and not punishment, and then do the one thing Peter says to do with your anxiety. Throw it on Him. He cares for you.

Father, I was born again into a living hope, and my inheritance is kept. Let me set my hope fully on Your grace, not on my circumstances. In the fire, remind me it is proving me, not punishing me. I cast this anxiety on You, because You care for me.

For your family

1 Peter 3:1 to 7 · 1 Peter 4:8 to 11

Peter takes the gospel into the house. He tells husbands and wives to live with gentleness, honor, and understanding, and he calls the hidden person of the heart precious in God's sight. Then he tells the whole church to keep loving one another earnestly, because love covers a multitude of sins, and to show hospitality without grumbling.

Pray this over the people in your home by name. For the gentle strength that does not have to win. For love that covers instead of keeping score. Especially over the one who is hardest to be patient with this week.

Lord, let this home be marked by gentle strength and honor. Teach us to love one another earnestly, the kind of love that covers and does not keep a record. Let the hidden person of the heart, quiet and unafraid, be what shapes this house. Whatever we do here, let us serve by the strength You supply, so that in everything You are glorified.

For Cleburne

1 Peter 2:9 to 12 · 1 Peter 3:15

Peter says you are a chosen race and a royal priesthood for a reason, that you may proclaim the excellencies of Him who called you out of darkness into His marvelous light. He tells exiles to keep their conduct honorable among outsiders, so that the very people watching them would end up glorifying God. And he says to always be ready to give a reason for the hope in you, with gentleness and respect.

So pray for the watching city. The neighbor, the coworker, the checkout line, the school pickup. Pray that your life would make someone ask, and that you would be ready with an answer that sounds like hope, because the hope in you is the hope of Cleburne too.

God, make my life among my neighbors honorable enough to raise a question. When someone asks about the hope in me, give me an answer carried with gentleness and respect. Open doors in Cleburne this week, and let this city see the One who called me out of darkness into His marvelous light.
