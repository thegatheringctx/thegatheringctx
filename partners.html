Friend,

You were in the room. You do not need a book to tell you what we studied. But I wanted you to hold it.

My heart is simple. The tools we are learning while we study together, I want in your hands. Not a summary. The thing itself. Something you can say out loud, and keep, and come back to.

These are not on the church website and they are not for sale. We made them for the people who stood with us. That is you.

Take the adult edition for yourself. Take the kids editions for your house. Give one away if the Lord puts somebody on your heart.
