Ages 4–7 · Philippians Week 1

Joy All the Time!

5 days with your family · Philippians 1

“I will rejoice. Yes, and I will rejoice.”

Philippians 1:18, ESV

Read together with your grown-up and earn +10 points each day!
1

Day 1

God Always Finishes What He Starts!

The Bible Says

“He who began a good work in you will bring it to completion at the day of Jesus Christ.”

Philippians 1:6, ESV

Have you ever started building something with blocks and then someone knocked it over before you could finish? That is so frustrating! But here is something amazing about God: He ALWAYS finishes what He starts.

A man named Paul wrote this verse from prison. He was in a jail cell, which is a hard, scary place to be. But even from jail, Paul knew something important. God started something good in him. And God was not going to stop working until it was completely done.

That is true for you too! God started something good in you the moment He made you. And He is still working on you every single day. He will not stop until the job is completely finished!

Let's Talk About It

  • Can you think of something you started and finished? How did it feel when it was done?
  • How does it feel to know God will never stop working on you?

Say It Out Loud!

God started something good in me and He will finish it! He never gives up on me! I walk with Jesus, and THAT means I win!

Amen!

Family Activity

Start drawing a picture together and do not finish it today. Come back tomorrow and finish it. Talk about how God is like that. He started you and He will always come back to finish the good work!

Parent Note

This is the foundational truth of Philippians 1:6. God's sanctifying work is ongoing and guaranteed. Help your child understand that God's investment in them is permanent, not conditional on their behavior.

2

Day 2

Paul Sang in Jail!

The Bible Says

“Always in every prayer of mine for you all making my prayer with joy.”

Philippians 1:4, ESV

A long time ago, Paul and his friend Silas were thrown into a really dark, cold jail. Their feet were locked in heavy chains. It hurt. It was scary. It was the middle of the night.

And do you know what they did? They sang! They sang songs to God right there in the dark. And then something amazing happened. An earthquake came! The doors flew open! The chains fell off!

The singing came BEFORE the miracle. Paul was not happy because everything was going well. Paul was happy because God was with him no matter what. That is real joy. Joy that does not need everything to be perfect first!

Let's Talk About It

  • When was a time things felt hard or scary? What helped you feel better?
  • Do you think you could sing to God even when things are hard?

Say It Out Loud!

I can have joy even when things are hard! God is with me no matter what! I walk with Jesus, and THAT means I win!

Amen!

Family Activity

Sing your favorite worship song together right now, even if nobody feels like it! See what happens to the room when you choose to sing to God!

Parent Note

The Acts 16 account is concrete and engaging for children. The miracle followed the praise. This plants the seed that worship is not a response to good circumstances but a weapon in hard ones.

3

Day 3

Even the Hard Stuff Can Help Other People!

The Bible Says

“What has happened to me has really served to advance the gospel.”

Philippians 1:12, ESV

While Paul was in jail, the guards had to be right next to him all day. They could not leave. And Paul used that time to tell them all about Jesus! The hard thing that happened to Paul, being put in jail, actually helped MORE people hear about God!

Sometimes when something hard happens to us, God uses it to help someone else. Maybe you got sick and a friend came to visit and you got to tell them about Jesus. Maybe something scary happened and your braveness helped another kid feel less scared.

God does not waste the hard things. He uses them!

Let's Talk About It

  • Has anything hard ever happened that ended up helping someone else?
  • Can you think of someone you could encourage this week?

Say It Out Loud!

God uses the hard things to help other people! My story can help someone else! I walk with Jesus, and THAT means I win!

Amen!

Family Activity

Write or draw a card for someone who is going through something hard right now. Tell them God loves them and does not waste hard things!

Parent Note

Paul's chains reached the Praetorian Guard. Help your child see that their difficulties, big or small, can become a testimony that points others to Jesus.

4

Day 4

Be Brave Like a Good Team!

The Bible Says

“Standing firm in one spirit, with one mind striving side by side for the faith of the gospel.”

Philippians 1:27, ESV

Paul told his friends: stand together! Stay brave together! Do not give up! He used a picture of soldiers standing side by side with their shields up, protecting each other. That sounds like a really good team!

Being a Christian is like being on the best team ever. We help each other. We cheer each other on. We do not let each other fall down alone. When one person on the team is scared, the others stand next to them and say: we are right here. You are not alone!

Your family is part of that team. Your church is part of that team. We stand together!

Let's Talk About It

  • Who is on your team? Who helps you stay brave?
  • Is there someone on your team who needs you to stand next to them this week?

Say It Out Loud!

I am part of God's team! I stand with my brothers and sisters! We are brave together! I walk with Jesus, and THAT means I win!

Amen!

Family Activity

Stand side by side with your family and link arms. Say together: "We stand together. We do not fall alone." Then pray for each person in your family by name.

Parent Note

The military image of striving side by side is perfect for this age. Community and belonging are core developmental needs. Help your child identify their people and pray for them specifically.

5

Day 5

Choose Joy Every Single Day!

The Bible Says

“I will rejoice. Yes, and I will rejoice.”

Philippians 1:18, ESV

Did you notice what Paul said? I WILL rejoice. He did not say I will rejoice if things get better. He did not say I will rejoice when I get out of jail. He said I WILL. It was a choice. He chose joy.

And then he said it again! Yes, and I will rejoice. Like he really meant it! Joy is not just a feeling that happens to you when everything is good. Joy is something you can CHOOSE. Because Jesus is always with you. And nothing can change that.

Every morning when you wake up, you can choose: today I will rejoice! Not because everything is perfect. But because Jesus is with you. That is the best reason of all.

Let's Talk About It

  • What is one thing you are happy about right now, even if today has been hard?
  • How can you choose joy tomorrow morning the moment you wake up?

Say It Out Loud!

I choose joy every day! Not because everything is perfect, but because Jesus is with me! I walk with Jesus, and THAT means I win!

Amen!

Family Prayer

Pray together: "God, thank You that joy is something we can choose. Help us choose joy tomorrow morning, even before anything has happened yet. You are good all the time. In Jesus' name, Amen!"

Parent Note

You made it through all five days! This week, start each morning with your child by asking: are you going to choose joy today? It builds a habit of volitional praise that will carry them through life.
