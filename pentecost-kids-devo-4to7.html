🔥 Ages 4–7 · Pentecost

The Fire Fell! 🔥

5 days with your family · Acts 2:1–4

“And they were all filled with the Holy Spirit.”

Acts 2:4, ESV

🔥 Read together with your grown-up and earn +10 points each day! 🔥
1

Day 1

God Made a Big Promise and He Kept It!

🌟

📖 The Bible Says

“I will pour out my Spirit on all flesh.”

Joel 2:28, ESV

A long, long time ago, before your great-great-great-great-grandparents were even born, God made a promise. He said: one day I am going to pour out my Holy Spirit on everyone. Not just on special people. On ALL people.

And do you know what? God kept that promise. On the very first Pentecost Sunday, the Holy Spirit came down from heaven like fire and wind. Everyone there felt it. Everyone. God always does what He says He will do!

💬 Let's Talk About It

  • Can you think of a promise someone kept for you? How did that feel?
  • Why is it so good to know that God always keeps His promises?

⚡ Say It Out Loud!

God always keeps His promises! The Holy Spirit is real and He is here! I walk with Jesus, and THAT means I win!

Amen! 🎉

✍️ Family Activity

Draw a picture of a big campfire with the word "PROMISE" written in the flames. God kept His promise with fire!

👁 Parent Note

Help your child understand that Pentecost is the birthday of the Church. God's promise in Joel 2 was fulfilled in Acts 2. God's timing is always perfect.

2

Day 2

The Fire and the Wind and the Sound!

💨

📖 The Bible Says

“And suddenly there came from heaven a sound like a mighty rushing wind, and it filled the entire house where they were sitting.”

Acts 2:2, ESV

Imagine sitting in a room with your friends. And then suddenly, WHOOOOSH! A sound like a giant wind fills the whole house. Then you see something amazing: little flames of fire that float and rest right on top of each person's head!

That is what happened on Pentecost Sunday! And here is a cool secret: in the Bible, the word for "wind" is also the word for "Spirit." So when that mighty wind came, it was God's Spirit arriving! The Holy Spirit is like the wind. You cannot see it, but you can feel it and see what it does!

💬 Let's Talk About It

  • Have you ever felt a big wind? What did it feel like?
  • If you could not see the Holy Spirit but you could feel Him, what do you think that would feel like?

⚡ Say It Out Loud!

The Holy Spirit is real just like the wind is real! I can feel God even when I cannot see Him! I walk with Jesus, and THAT means I win!

Amen! 🎉

✍️ Family Activity

Blow on your hand. Feel the air? Now blow on a piece of paper and watch it move. The Holy Spirit is like that. You cannot see Him, but He is real and He moves things!

👁 Parent Note

John 3:8 uses the same wind-Spirit connection. Jesus told Nicodemus "the wind blows where it wishes and you hear its sound." This is a beautiful biblical metaphor, not metaphysics. Keep it concrete and fun.

3

Day 3

God Put Special Power Inside You!

⚡

📖 The Bible Says

“You will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you.”

Acts 1:8, ESV

Before Jesus went back to heaven, He told His friends something really important. He said: I am going to send the Holy Spirit, and when He comes, you will have POWER!

Not power to fly like a superhero. But power to love people, to be brave, to tell people about Jesus, and to do hard things you could not do on your own. The Holy Spirit is like a battery that never runs out! And the amazing thing is, if you love Jesus, that power already lives inside you right now!

💬 Let's Talk About It

  • Can you think of something hard that you needed help doing?
  • How does it feel to know God's power is already inside you?

⚡ Say It Out Loud!

God's power lives inside me! The Holy Spirit helps me be brave and kind! I walk with Jesus, and THAT means I win!

Amen! 🎉

✍️ Family Activity

Find a flashlight or a nightlight. When it is off, it cannot help anyone. When it is ON and plugged in, it shines! The Holy Spirit is like being plugged into God's power. Draw a shining light!

👁 Parent Note

Affirm that the Holy Spirit's power is for everyday life. Not just big moments. Help your child name one specific situation this week where they can depend on the Spirit's help.

4

Day 4

You Are Never Ever Alone!

🤝

📖 The Bible Says

“And I will ask the Father, and he will give you another Helper, to be with you forever.”

John 14:16, ESV

Have you ever felt scared or alone? Maybe in the dark at night, or at a new place where you did not know anyone? Jesus knows that feeling. And He made a promise: I will send you a Helper who will ALWAYS be with you.

That Helper is the Holy Spirit! He is not your friend for just one day. He is not your friend just at church. He is with you FOREVER. At school. At the grocery store. In your bedroom. When you are happy and when you are sad. He is always, always there. You are never alone.

💬 Let's Talk About It

  • Is there a place where you sometimes feel scared or alone?
  • How does it feel to know the Holy Spirit is there with you in that place right now?

⚡ Say It Out Loud!

The Holy Spirit is always with me! I am never alone! God is my Helper forever! I walk with Jesus, and THAT means I win!

Amen! 🎉

✍️ Family Activity

Draw a picture of a place where you sometimes feel alone or scared. Then draw a big bright light or flame right next to you in that place to represent the Holy Spirit being there with you.

👁 Parent Note

Pray with your child specifically about the place they mentioned. Let them hear you talk to the Holy Spirit as a Person who is present. This builds the foundation for a real prayer life.

5

Day 5

You Are the Church. The Fire Lives in YOU!

⛰️

📖 The Bible Says

“Do you not know that you are God's temple and that God's Spirit dwells in you?”

1 Corinthians 3:16, ESV

A long time ago, God lived in a special building called the Temple. Only certain people could get close to where God was. But then Jesus changed EVERYTHING.

Now God does not live in a building. God lives in people. In YOU, if you love Jesus! You are God's temple. That means everywhere you go today, you carry God's presence with you. To school. To the park. To your neighbor's house. Wherever you go, the fire goes with you!

The Church is not just the building we meet in on Sundays. The Church is all of us together. We are all carrying the fire. And together we let it shine!

💬 Let's Talk About It

  • Where are you going this week where you can bring the fire?
  • What is one way you can let God's love shine through you this week?

⚡ Say It Out Loud!

I am God's temple! The Holy Spirit lives in me! Wherever I go the fire goes with me! I walk with Jesus, and THAT means I win!

Amen! 🎉

✍️ Family Prayer

Pray together: "Holy Spirit, thank You for living in us. Thank You that we are never alone. Help us shine Your light wherever we go this week. In Jesus' name, Amen!"

👁 Parent Note

You made it through all five days! This week send your child out each morning with: "Remember, the fire goes with you." It is a simple declaration that builds identity as a Spirit-carrier.
