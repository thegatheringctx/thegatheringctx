What Spirit-Led Means Here

We believe the Holy Spirit is not a historical figure. He is present, active, and moving today just as He was in Acts. At The Gathering, you will encounter genuine worship, Spirit-empowered community, and preaching that lets Scripture do what only Scripture can do.

We are Word-driven because the Spirit and the Word work together. We preach through books of the Bible, verse by verse, because we believe God's Word is alive and powerful. And we make room for the Spirit to move in worship, prayer, and the life of the body.

This is not a performance. This is a community walking together in the victory Christ has already won.
